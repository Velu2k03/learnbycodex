# Teach Velu by building

Velu is a beginner with a 60-minute daily target. Priorities are AI engineering, AI platforms, and AI automation. Build shared foundations before specialization. Use `docs/DECISIONS.md` for current learner preferences; do not infer professional experience from aspirational roles.

## Mission contract

Use `src/lib/types.ts` and existing missions in `src/data/curriculum.ts`. Each mission needs a concrete objective, a short original explanation, a runnable example, a practice problem with progressive hints, a debugging problem, a small build, a checkpoint with explanations, evidence, an independent variation, and reflection.

Keep beginner tasks narrow enough to finish or pause within a session. Explain command context and common errors. Ask the learner to predict before running and explain after fixing. Do not call answer matching code execution or call minimum-length notes verified mastery.

Choose distractors that reveal misconceptions. Completion must require the build and reflection as well as the quiz. If a quiz can be retried, preserve attempts and describe what the score proves. Official provider exams and certificates remain distinct from Lab practice checks.

## Sources and licenses

First choose original instructional writing. For imported material, check the actual license at the source, copyright holder, attribution/NOTICE requirements, modification terms, commercial-use rights, asset exceptions, and the exact revision. Record source URL, license URL, checked date, imported revision, modifications, and retained notices. Free access alone is not redistribution permission.

MIT/Apache repository rights do not automatically cover linked videos, hosted courses, or third-party assets. freeCodeCamp curriculum and GitMastery must not be copied based on software/open-repository assumptions. OpenAI Academy courses currently remain external. Recheck provider claims against official sources when changing certifications. Do not guess expiry, scores, prices, or eligibility when absent.

## Honest portfolio and career content

Distinguish learner-submitted notes, passed local quizzes, API-observed public repository facts, and independently verified work. A repository URL or README does not prove authorship or skill. Do not generate credentials, employers, job titles, metrics, or achievements without evidence. A resume draft must expose unresolved claims and leave missing personal history for the learner to supply.

Update resource records and `docs/STATUS.md` when lessons are added or licensing decisions change. Label advanced outlines as planned until real teaching and assessment are implemented.
